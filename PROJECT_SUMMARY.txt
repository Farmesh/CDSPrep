## 📞 SUPPORT

### Common Questions

**Q: Do I need internet?**  
A: Only for first-time download. Works offline after.

**Q: Where is my data stored?**  
A: Locally in your browser (localStorage). Completely private.

**Q: Can I use on multiple devices?**  
A: Yes, but progress syncs separately on each device.

**Q: What if I clear browser data?**  
A: Your progress will be lost. Export regularly to backup.

**Q: Can I add more questions?**  
A: Yes! Edit `assets/js/questions.js` and add new questions.
